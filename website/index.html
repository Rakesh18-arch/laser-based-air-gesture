AIRWRITE GESTURE LANGUAGE

Control the board
with one hand.

Inspired by modern camera-first spatial drawing workflows such as AirVA, adapted to AIRWRITE’s Windows Smart Board and existing laser tools. Use the gesture launcher to open the laptop or USB webcam.

☝Index pointerMove the cursor and hover over tools.
🤏Thumb + index pinchDraw in Pen / Smart Write; pinch and release to click or select. Other fingers can stay naturally curled.
✌V-sign panRaise index and middle fingers to pan the board canvas.
↕Thumb + middle pinchMove your hand up to zoom in and down to zoom out. Keep the index away from the thumb.
☰Three fingers raisedHold to open the radial tools menu; point to an option, wait for its highlight, then pinch and release to choose.
◎Primary fingertip trackingOne stable hand is tracked; only the index fingertip is the pointer. Other fingertips are gesture landmarks only; no palm-state gate is used.
One gesture menu. The complete Smart Board.

After the three-finger menu opens, point to an option, let it highlight, then pinch and release. Only the index fingertip moves the pointer; other fingertips classify gestures. Pan and menu poses need agreement in 3 of the latest 5 frames. The hand menu can reach Cursor, Drag, Pen, Smart Write, Swipe Type, Eraser, Shapes, Scroll, color and brush size, page navigation, Undo, Redo, Save PNG, Export PDF, Clear page and Close Board.
